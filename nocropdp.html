What NoCrop DP does

NoCrop DP fits a photo you choose into a square profile picture without cropping it, filling the empty space with a blur of the same photo, a solid colour, or a gradient. The finished image is saved to your device's Pictures/NoCropDP folder.

Your photos stay on your device

  • Photos are picked by you through the Android photo picker and are processed entirely on your device. No photo is ever uploaded, sent, or shared by the app.
  • The app has no account system, no sign-in, and no server of its own.
  • The app does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or any personal information.

Advertising

NoCrop DP shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and use an advertising identifier and related device data as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: used only so AdMob can load ads.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below only): used only to save the DP image you created into your Pictures folder. On Android 10 and above, no storage permission is needed or requested.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.

Children

NoCrop DP is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13.
