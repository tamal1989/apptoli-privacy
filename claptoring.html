What ClapToRing does

ClapToRing helps you find your phone: when you turn the finder on, it

listens for a clap or a whistle and then rings loudly — even when the

phone is on silent — with vibration and a flashing light, until you tap

"TAP TO STOP".

Microphone use — analysed on-device only

  • ClapToRing uses the microphone only while the finder is switched

on, to detect claps and whistles.

  • The audio is analysed on your device, in real time, in memory.
  • Nothing is recorded. Nothing is stored. Nothing is uploaded.

There is no audio file, no transcript, and no copy of any sound —

each moment of audio is checked for a clap/whistle pattern and

immediately discarded.

  • When the finder is off, the microphone is not used at all.
  • A small on-screen notification is shown the whole time the finder is

listening, and you can stop it at any moment from that notification

or from the app.

Data stored on your device

The only information ClapToRing keeps is stored locally on your

phone:

  • Your settings (detection mode, sensitivity, melody choice, ring

length and volume, vibration and flashlight choices, language).

  • A history list of detections (the time a clap or whistle was heard,

and whether you tapped "Found"). This list holds at most 50 entries,

never leaves your device, and you can clear it at any time from the

History screen.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

What we collect

Nothing. ClapToRing has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and

no server of ours. We do not collect, receive, sell, or share any

personal data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

ClapToRing shows ads provided by Google AdMob (one banner on the home

screen and an occasional full-screen ad). To serve and measure ads,

Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID and

approximate, non-precise data as described in Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your

device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Permissions, and why each is requested

  • Microphone — to detect your clap or whistle (on-device only, see

above).

  • Notifications — to show the "listening" notification and the

full-screen ringing alert.

  • Full-screen intent — so the ringing screen can appear over the

lock screen when your phone is found.

  • Vibrate — to vibrate during the alert, if you enable it.
  • Modify audio settings — to raise the alarm volume to your chosen

level during an alert (your previous volume is restored afterwards),

so the ring is heard even on silent.

  • Flashlight — to blink the torch during an alert, if you enable

it. The camera is never used to take photos or video.

  • Internet / network state — used only by the AdMob ads SDK.

Children

ClapToRing is a general utility app and is not directed at children

under 13.
