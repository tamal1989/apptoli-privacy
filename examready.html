Privacy Policy — ExamReady

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

ExamReady ("the app"), by AppToli, resizes photos, signatures, thumb

impressions and handwritten declarations to the exact pixel and KB size

that Indian exam portals (SSC, UPSC, IBPS, SBI, Railways, NEET, JEE and

others) demand. This policy explains what happens to your information when

you use the app.

The short version

Your photos never leave your phone. The app has no account, no login

and no server of ours. Every photo is processed entirely on your device,

and the finished file is saved to your own gallery

(Pictures/FormResizer).

Information stored on your device

The app stores, on your device only:

  • The exam and document type you last selected
  • A short list of the files you made (name, size, dimensions and date),

shown in the app's "My Files" section

  • Your language preference

This data is used solely to run the app. It is never transmitted to us or

to any third party by the app itself.

Photos

Photos are accessed only through the system photo picker, only for the

image you choose, and only when you choose it. The app does not scan your

gallery. Resizing, cropping and compression all happen on the device; no

photo is uploaded anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may

collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, approximate

location (derived from IP address), and app interaction data, in accordance

with Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

AdMob's data collection is separate from your photos: the images you

process and the files you create are never sent to AdMob or to us.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device

settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — used only by the AdMob ads SDK.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below only) — used only to save your finished

file into your gallery's Pictures/FormResizer folder.

The app does not request access to your contacts, messages, calls or

location.

Children

The app is a general utility for exam applicants and is not directed at

children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same
