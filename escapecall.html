What Escape Call does

Escape Call lets you schedule a simulated incoming call — with a caller name, photo and ringtone you choose — so you can gracefully exit a boring meeting, a bad date, or any situation. The call is simulated entirely by the app on your device. It is not a real phone call, and the app cannot make or receive real calls.

Your data stays on your device

  • The caller name, photo and ringtone you pick are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, so your scheduled call looks the way you set it up. They are never uploaded, sent, or shared.
  • The app has no account system, no sign-in, and no server of its own.
  • The app does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, call history, or any personal information. It never accesses your real contacts or call log.

Advertising

Escape Call shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and use an advertising identifier and related device data as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Permissions

  • Exact alarms: so your escape call rings at the exact minute you scheduled.
  • Full-screen intent & notifications: so the simulated incoming call can appear on your screen, including over the lock screen.
  • Vibrate: the phone vibrates while the simulated call rings, like a real call.
  • Internet / network state: used only so AdMob can load ads.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.

Children

Escape Call is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13.
