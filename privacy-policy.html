What Dog Whistle does

Dog Whistle plays high-frequency tones (8,000–22,000 Hz) through your phone's speaker. Dogs can hear these tones even though most adults cannot. You can use them for calling or training your dog.

No data collected

  • Dog Whistle has no account system, no sign-in, and no server of its own.
  • The app does not use the microphone and records nothing.
  • The app does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or any personal information. Nothing you do in the app is uploaded anywhere.

Advertising

Dog Whistle shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and use an advertising identifier and related device data as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: used only so AdMob can load ads.

A note on animal welfare

High-frequency sounds are powerful for dogs. Use short bursts, keep the phone away from your dog's ears, watch your dog's reaction, and stop if your dog seems stressed or uncomfortable.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.

Dog Whistle is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13.
