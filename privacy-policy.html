Privacy Policy — Vibration Meter

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Vibration Meter is developed by AppToli. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

What the app does

Vibration Meter measures vibration using your phone's built-in motion sensor (accelerometer) and shows the readings as a live graph with current, maximum and average values. All readings are processed on your device, in real time. Nothing about your measurements is stored after you close the app, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-up and no analytics of our own.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and share certain data, including:

  • Approximate location
  • App interactions
  • Diagnostics
  • Device or other IDs (including the Android Advertising ID)

This data is collected and shared by Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, as described in Google's own policies. Data sent by the app is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your device's Google settings.

Sensor data

The app uses the accelerometer only while you are measuring. Readings are processed on the device and are never recorded, saved or transmitted by us.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and above.
