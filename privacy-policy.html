What Push-Up Counter does

Push-Up Counter counts your push-ups using your phone's built-in proximity sensor while the phone lies flat on the floor, and keeps a simple record of your sets: daily totals, your best set, your day streak and a short history of finished sets. All of this is stored only on your device, inside the app.

No data collected by us

  • Counting happens on your device. Your workout record stays in the app's private storage on your phone — it is never uploaded anywhere.
  • The app has no account system, no sign-in, and no server of its own.
  • The app does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or any personal information.

Advertising

Push-Up Counter shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and use an advertising identifier and related device data as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: used only so AdMob can load ads.
  • The proximity sensor is a built-in hardware sensor and needs no permission.

Health note

Push-Up Counter is a simple counting tool for general fitness. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical or health advice. Exercise within your own limits.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.

Children

Push-Up Counter is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13.
