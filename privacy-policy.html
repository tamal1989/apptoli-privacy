What Speedometer GPS does

Speedometer GPS uses your phone's GPS to show your current speed on a big dial, along with your maximum speed, trip distance and trip time. It is made for car, bike and train journeys.

Location stays on your device

  • Your location is used only while tracking is running, to calculate speed and distance on the device itself.
  • Your location, routes and trips are never uploaded, stored on a server, or shared. The app has no server of its own.
  • The app has no account system and no sign-in.
  • The app does not collect your name, email address, contacts, or any personal information.

Advertising

Speedometer GPS shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and use an advertising identifier and related device data as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Note that AdMob's own data collection (for example, approximate location derived from IP address for ad serving) is governed by Google's policy, not by AppToli. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Permissions

  • Location (fine & coarse): the core function — reading GPS speed and distance while tracking runs.
  • Internet / network state: used only so AdMob can load ads.

Safety

Never interact with your phone while driving. Mount the phone safely before you set off, and let a passenger make any changes.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.

Children

Speedometer GPS is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13.
