What Truth or Lie is

Truth or Lie is a prank app for fun with friends. A friend holds a finger on the "scanner", the app runs a short simulated scan, and then dramatically declares TRUTH or LIE. The scan is entirely simulated — the app does not measure anything about the person, and it cannot detect lies. The verdict is pre-set by the person holding the phone (the "scanner mood" on the home screen).

No data collected

  • The app reads no biometric data. The "fingerprint scan" is an animation that responds to touch only — no fingerprint, pulse, or any other body measurement is captured or stored.
  • The app has no account system, no sign-in, and no server of its own.
  • The app does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or any personal information. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Advertising

Truth or Lie shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and use an advertising identifier and related device data as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: a short buzz when the verdict appears.
  • Internet / network state: used only so AdMob can load ads.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.

Children

Truth or Lie is a general-purpose entertainment app and is not directed at children under 13.
