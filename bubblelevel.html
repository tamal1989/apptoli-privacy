Privacy Policy — Bubble Level

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Bubble Level is developed by AppToli. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

What the app does

Bubble Level uses your phone's built-in motion sensor (accelerometer) to show a surface vial and a flat bullseye level with angle readings. All readings are processed on your device, in real time. Nothing about your measurements is stored or uploaded anywhere. A calibration you set is kept only in the app's on-device preferences.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-up and no analytics of our own.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and share certain data, including:

  • Approximate location
  • App interactions
  • Diagnostics
  • Device or other IDs (including the Android Advertising ID)

This data is collected and shared by Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, as described in Google's own policies. Data sent by the app is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your device's Google settings.

Sensor data

The app uses the accelerometer only while the level screen is open. Readings are processed on the device and are never recorded or transmitted by us.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and above.
