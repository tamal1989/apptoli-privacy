Privacy Policy — SpeakerClean

By AppToli · Contact: xyztamal123@proton.me · Last updated: 22 November 2026

SpeakerClean ("the app") plays special tones that push water and dust out of your phone speaker. The app works fully offline and is designed to collect as little data as possible.

What the app does on your device

  • The app generates and plays cleaning tones using your phone's speaker, and can vibrate the phone and raise the media volume while a clean is running (both can be turned off in Settings).
  • Your settings (language, vibration, volume boost) are stored only on your device.
  • The app does not record audio, access your files, contacts, photos, or location, and it works without an account or sign-in.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use a device advertising identifier and related technical data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). No other third-party services are used.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone. Because the app keeps its settings only on your device, we have no user data to share.

Children

The app is a general utility tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new "last updated" date.
