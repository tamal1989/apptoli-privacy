What Stud & Wire Finder does

Stud & Wire Finder uses your phone's built-in magnetic sensor to detect metal hidden in walls — screws, nails, pipes and metal studs — and helps you check a spot before you drill. Readings are shown live on the meter; the beeps get faster as you get closer to metal.

No data collected

  • Sensor readings are processed on your device, in real time. Nothing is recorded, stored, or uploaded.
  • The app has no account system, no sign-in, and no server of its own.
  • The app does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or any personal information.

Advertising

Stud & Wire Finder shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and use an advertising identifier and related device data as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: a short buzz when metal is found.
  • Internet / network state: used only so AdMob can load ads.

Accuracy note

A phone's magnetic sensor detects metal only. Wooden studs without metal parts, and wires carrying no current, may not register. Readings vary by phone and wall material — always double-check before drilling, and treat this app as a guide, not a guarantee.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.
