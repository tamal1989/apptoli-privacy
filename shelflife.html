Privacy Policy — ShelfLife

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

ShelfLife ("the app"), by AppToli, helps you remember expiry dates of medicines,

food, documents and household items. This policy explains what happens to

your information when you use the app.

The short version

Your items, dates, notes and photos never leave your phone. The app has

no account, no login and no server of ours. Everything you enter is stored

only in the app's private storage on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app stores, on your device only:

  • Item names, categories, expiry dates, quantities and notes you type in
  • Reminder preferences you choose
  • Photos you optionally attach to an item (copied into the app's private

storage on your device)

This data is used solely to show your list and to fire your reminders. It is

never transmitted to us or to any third party by the app itself. If you use

the Backup feature, the backup file is created only where you choose to

save it (for example, your own Google Drive or files app) — that choice and

that file are entirely under your control.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may

collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, approximate

location (derived from IP address), and app interaction data, in accordance

with Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

AdMob's data collection is separate from your expiry items: the contents

of your list (item names, dates, notes, photos) are never sent to AdMob or

to us.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device

settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only to show your expiry reminders.
  • Exact alarms — used only so reminders arrive at the time you chose.
  • Internet / network state — used only by the AdMob ads SDK.

The app does not request access to your contacts, messages, calls, location

or files. Photos are accessed only through the system photo picker when you

choose to attach one.

Children

The app is a general household utility and is not directed at children

under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same

address with a new "last updated" date.
