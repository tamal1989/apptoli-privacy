Privacy Policy — ChargeGuard

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

ChargeGuard ("the app") is developed by AppToli. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What the app does

ChargeGuard watches your phone's battery level while it charges and rings an alarm when the level you chose is reached. All monitoring and the alarm happen entirely on your device.

Data we collect

We collect no personal data. Specifically:

  • The app does not require an account or sign-in.
  • Battery information (level, charging state, temperature, voltage) is read on your phone only, to show the gauge and to decide when to ring. It is never uploaded, shared, or stored beyond the app's own settings on your phone.
  • The app works fully offline except for loading ads.

Permissions

  • Foreground service + notifications — so the charge watch can keep running while you charge and show its status and the full-charge alarm.
  • Full-screen intent — to show the alarm screen over the lock screen when your charge target is reached.
  • Vibrate — optional vibration with the alarm.
  • Battery optimisation exemption (optional, requested only if you tap the button) — so the phone does not stop the watch in the background.
  • Internet / network state — only so the ads library can load ads.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use a device advertising identifier and related data to serve and measure ads, as described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Children

The app is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new "last updated" date.
