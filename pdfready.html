Privacy Policy — PDF Ready

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

PDF Ready ("the app"), by AppToli, converts photos into PDF files with

exact KB size control for portal uploads, and also merges PDFs and

exports PDF pages as images. This policy explains what happens to your

information when you use the app.

The short version

Your photos and PDFs never leave your phone. The app has no account,

no login and no server of ours. Every photo and PDF is processed entirely

on your device, and finished files are saved to your own storage

(Documents/PDFReady for PDFs, Pictures/PDFReady for exported images).

Information stored on your device

The app stores, on your device only:

  • A short list of the PDFs you made (name, page count, size and date),

shown in the app's "My PDFs" section

  • Your language preference and default page size

This data is used solely to run the app. It is never transmitted to us or

to any third party by the app itself.

Photos and documents

Photos are accessed only through the system photo picker, only for the

images you choose, and only when you choose them. The app does not scan

your gallery. PDF files are opened only when you pick them (for Merge

PDF or PDF to Images). Conversion, compression and rendering all happen

on the device; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may

collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, approximate

location (derived from IP address), and app interaction data, in accordance

with Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

AdMob's data collection is separate from your files: the photos and

PDFs you process are never sent to AdMob or to us.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device

settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — used only by the AdMob ads SDK.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below only) — used only to save your finished

files into the PDFReady folders.

  • The camera is used only through the system camera app when you tap

"Scan Document"; the app itself does not hold a camera permission.

The app does not request access to your contacts, messages, calls or

location.

Children

The app is a general document utility and is not directed at children

under 13.
