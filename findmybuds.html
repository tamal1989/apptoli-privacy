Privacy Policy — FindMyBuds

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

FindMyBuds ("the app") is developed by AppToli. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What the app does

FindMyBuds helps you locate lost Bluetooth Low Energy devices (earbuds, watches, speakers, tags) by measuring their signal strength on your phone. All scanning and signal processing happens entirely on your device.

Data we collect

We collect no personal data. Specifically:

  • The app does not require an account or sign-in.
  • Bluetooth scan results (device names, addresses, signal strength) are processed in memory on your phone and are never uploaded, shared, or stored beyond the devices you choose to star, which are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • The app does not track, use, or store your location. On older Android versions (Android 11 and below), the system requires the Location permission and Location services to be on for Bluetooth scanning to work; the app never reads your location from this. On Android 12+, the app declares that Bluetooth scans are never used to derive location.
  • The app works fully offline except for loading ads.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_SCAN / BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, or BLUETOOTH + Location on older Android) — to scan for and list nearby Bluetooth devices.
  • Vibrate — optional short pulses when a device is very close.
  • Internet / network state — only so the ads library can load ads.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use a device advertising identifier and related data to serve and measure ads, as described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Children

The app is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new "last updated" date.
