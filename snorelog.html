What SnoreLog does

SnoreLog listens through your phone's microphone while you sleep and saves short audio clips of the moments when real sound occurs (for example snoring or sleep-talking), so you can play them back in the morning. Quiet periods are analysed on the device and are not saved.

Your recordings stay on your device

  • Clips are stored only in the app's private folder on your phone. They are never uploaded, sent, or shared by the app — sharing happens only when you tap the share button yourself.
  • The app has no account system, no sign-in, and no server of its own.
  • The app does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or any personal information.
  • You can delete any clip at any time from inside the app; deleting the app deletes all clips with it.

Microphone use

The microphone is used only while the night watch is running — that is, after you tap START LISTENING and until you tap STOP. A persistent notification is shown the whole time the microphone is active. SnoreLog is not a medical device and does not diagnose sleep conditions.

Advertising

SnoreLog shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and use an advertising identifier and related device data as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Permissions

  • Microphone: the core function — listening for night sounds while the watch runs.
  • Notifications: shows the ongoing "listening" notification while the watch runs.
  • Internet / network state: used only so AdMob can load ads.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data or your recordings. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.

Children

SnoreLog is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13.
